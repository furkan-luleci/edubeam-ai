## EduBeam AI Modifications

This fork contains substantial modifications and extensions to the original EduBeam project, including multimodal AI-assisted structural model generation, structural-analysis education tools, expanded unit handling, and additional modeling capabilities.

The AI-enhanced modifications in this fork were developed by **Furkan Lüleci**.

Major additions include:

- Gemini-powered AI Model Builder
- Structural diagram image upload
- Clipboard image pasting with Ctrl+V
- Selectable Gemini vision models
- Automatic recognition of structural geometry
- Automatic recognition of nodes and members
- Automatic recognition of supports and internal hinges
- Automatic recognition of nodal, concentrated member, and distributed loads
- Automatic recognition of SI and U.S. customary units
- Automatic switching of EduBeam display units to match the source problem
- Engineering coordinate convention with positive global z upward
- Expanded U.S. customary unit support, including kip and ksi
- Correct force-per-length conversion for distributed loads
- Member-specific Young's modulus E
- Member-specific shear modulus G
- Member-specific cross-sectional area A
- Member-specific moment of inertia I
- Member-specific cross-section height h
- Thermal expansion coefficient alpha support
- Temperature-load recognition
- Celsius and Fahrenheit temperature-change handling
- Shear-force equation display for individual structural members
- Bending-moment equation display for individual structural members
